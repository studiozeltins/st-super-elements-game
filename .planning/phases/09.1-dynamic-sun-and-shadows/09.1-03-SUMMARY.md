## Task 2 — In-game verification gate

### FPS gate — SHADOW-03 (D-14) — PASS (real measurement)

`scripts/fps_playtest.py` drove the built client (`vite preview :4173`) against the local
world: logged in as `perfbot`, granted the maxed loadout, walked to camp 5, force-spawned
all 3 goliath sizes, and sampled every `requestAnimationFrame` delta over a 60 s fight.
Run once with the moving sun and once with `FPS_QUERY=?nomovingsun` (the byte-exact frozen
baseline).

| Run | Frames | Avg FPS | 1% low | Worst frame |
|-----|--------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Moving sun (default) | 3517 | 58.5 | 30.0 | 333 ms (single GC hitch) |
| Frozen baseline (`?nomovingsun`) | 3556 | 59.3 | 31.1 | 66 ms |

**Verdict: no regression.** Δ avg −0.8 FPS (1.3 %), Δ 1%-low −1.1 FPS (3.5 %) — both inside
run-to-run noise (the two fights had different death counts: 5 vs 1, and different RNG). The
moving sun's per-frame cost is the ~3 cross-product basis rebuild; it rides the existing 30 Hz
shadow throttle (`createPixelRenderer` `shadowMap.autoUpdate=false`, unchanged), exactly as
RESEARCH predicted. The lone 333 ms worst frame in the moving run is a single garbage-collection
outlier, not a systematic cost (the 1%-lows are effectively equal). Both runs also exercised the
full new renderer wiring end-to-end in a real rendered client without crashing.
