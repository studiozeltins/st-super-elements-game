<objective>
Provide the pure-math foundation for the flag UAT gap fixes (tests 4/5/6/9): a downwind

discipline: zero-import helpers test-first, THREE wrapper second).

`?nowind` limp-hang contract (D-12) captured as an objective unit test instead of a
visual-only truth.
