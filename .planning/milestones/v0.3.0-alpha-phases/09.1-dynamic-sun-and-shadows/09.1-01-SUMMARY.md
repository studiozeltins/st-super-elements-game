## Decisions Made
- **Peak = 54.204°, not 75°** (RESEARCH A1): pinned `ELEV_PEAK_DEG` to the exact SUN_OFFSET elevation so the moving arc passes through the D-03 midday key and the `?nomovingsun` fallback shows no jump. A test rejects any drift from this value.
- **Sun-position sign convention** (RESEARCH OQ1): `sunDir` returns the direction toward the sun (matches SUN_OFFSET); `buildSunBasis` negates internally for the light-travel Gram-Schmidt basis. Locked by the noon basis-equality test against the shipped `sunRight`/`sunUp`.
